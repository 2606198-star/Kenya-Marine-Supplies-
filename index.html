History of Kenya Marine Supplies Ltd

Services Offered

The company provides marine transport services such as passenger travel, cargo movement and boat hire. These services help people cross waterways and transport goods between places. Marine transport can be useful in coastal areas and around Lake Victoria.

Products Supplied

Kenya Marine Supplies Ltd supplies marine products such as fishing boats, passenger boats, life jackets, anchors, fishing nets, navigation equipment and boat engines. These products support fishing, safe boating and other activities carried out on water.

Importance of Lakes and Oceans in Marine Transport

Lakes and oceans support marine transport by providing waterways for boats and ships to carry people and goods. In Kenya, the Indian Ocean and Lake Victoria are important for transport, fishing and tourism. Water transport can connect communities and help move supplies.
