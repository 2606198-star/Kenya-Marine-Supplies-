Welcome to Kenya Marine Supplies Ltd

Welcome to Kenya Marine Supplies Ltd. We provide boats, marine transport services and aquatic equipment for fishermen, boat operators, tourism businesses and other customers in Kenya.

Our Main Activities

Our activities include selling fishing boats, passenger boats and speedboats. We also provide passenger transport, cargo transport and boat hire services. We supply life jackets, anchors, fishing nets, navigation equipment and boat engines.
