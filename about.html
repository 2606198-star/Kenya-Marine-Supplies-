Who We Are

Kenya Marine Supplies Ltd is a Kenyan company that specializes in selling boats, providing marine transport services and supplying marine and aquatic equipment. The establishment year was not provided in the assignment brief.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide useful boats, dependable transport services and quality marine equipment to customers in Kenya.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted supplier of marine products and services for customers along the Kenyan coast and around Lake Victoria.

Our Operations in Kenya

We serve fishermen, boat operators, tourism businesses and shipping companies. Our work supports fishing, tourism, passenger movement and the transport of goods by water.
